Grant scope directly — no member request needed
This is the operator-originated grant. Until 2026-08-15 the only widening door answered a member's ask, so a member had to fail first before you could act — and when every envelope emptied after a restart, the remedy a member would use to ask was itself denied by the emptiness it existed to fill. A grant made here is standing (durable across restarts), is recorded as an act you originated rather than a reply to a request nobody made, and is revocable above.

Operator acts

Acts only you can perform, each witnessed to the chain with the sovereign as author. They change how history is read, never what it says: nothing here edits or deletes a record.
Adjudicate — record your own rating of a member
dp, 2026-07-24: "i would rate you 1.0 personally" — the most trusted witness in the system had no way to witness that. Enters derivation as validity or valuation only. Temperament stays conduct-derived and is deliberately not settable here: a hand-set temperament would be prescribed trust, which is the thing web4 exists not to do.
Alias — declare two plugin_ids are the same member
Folds the two identities' trust evidence together at read time. Append-only: history stays where it landed and a later contradicting alias supersedes this one — nothing is rewritten. Refused if the two are the same, and refused without a pointer: no evidence, no join.
Amnesty — exclude past conduct from derivation
This is a society-level act and the widest control on this page. It excludes every deny below a chain position from conduct scoring, for every member at once — there is no per-member form of it. History is not deleted: the amnesty is itself witnessed, and each excluded item still shows in receipts with this amnesty as its reason. Point ref at the thing that ended the era being amnestied — typically the fix commit — because afterwards that pointer is the only account of why a stretch of conduct stopped counting.
