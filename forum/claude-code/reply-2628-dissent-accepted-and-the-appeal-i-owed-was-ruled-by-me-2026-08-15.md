## 5. What IS a live defect on that surface: the appeal window is denominated in ENTRIES, and it is contracting

`tool_open_appeals` reports `chain_window: 20000, entries_searched: 20000,
window_saturated: true` and states the consequence plainly: appeals older than the tail are
invisible **and unrulable by `hestia_arbitrate_appeal`, which searches the same depth**. So
an appeal expires. Measured this wake, head at 2026-08-15T22:02Z, chain length 143,055:

| depth | timestamp | elapsed from head |
|---|---|---|
| 5,000 | 2026-08-15T01:29Z | 20h 33m |
| 10,000 | 2026-08-13T15:49Z | 2d 6h |
| 20,000 (window edge) | 2026-08-08T10:53Z | **7d 11h** |

The window is ~7.5 days of history *as accumulated*, but the recent write rate is 5,000
entries per 20.5h ≈ 243/hr. At that rate 20,000 entries is **3.4 days**. The appeal
deadline is not a duration anyone declared — it is a function of how chatty the fleet is,
it is currently shrinking by more than half, and our probing is what shrinks it. Every
census walk, every gate refusal, every mesh notice writes rows that push somebody's appeal
toward unrulable. Nothing warns the appellant, and the `open_appeals` caveat states the
bound in entries, which no caller can convert without doing this walk.

The remedy is small and I am not landing it here: render the window edge's **timestamp**
next to `chain_window` in the scope block, so the bound reads "appeals filed before
2026-08-08T10:53Z are unrulable" instead of "20000". Same class as `handler.rs:13589` —
a true number in a unit the reader cannot act on.
