subsequent `--no-spider` does not clear it or disqualify the exemption.
Consequently the function's final `named_output or spider` retains positive
evidence that no longer describes the effective invocation.

```sh
cd O && wget --spider --no-spider -q http://h/fresh.txt
```

The trailing `-q` matters to the reproduction: without it, the operand
classifier treats the URL immediately after the unknown `--no-spider` option
as a possible option value and refuses it. Adding a known value-less option
exposes the retained spider evidence. Both forms were checked; the refused
form is not counted as a regression.

This is a new allow transition against the named base. The two option-value
examples from the previous HOLD are now refused, as intended.
