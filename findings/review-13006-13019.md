Source reading confirms the mechanism claims: `Closure.match()` builds
candidates raw + cwd-joined + **realpath'd** and matches the union
(`hestia_governance_closure.py:223-249`). Therefore:

- A link that **already exists** is caught (realpath of the candidate
  resolves into the closure) — P1/P2/E1.
- A link **created earlier in the same call** is not (realpath runs before
  the link exists; the `ln` names the governed dir only in read position)
  — F1/F2. The exposure is same-call-only, exactly as #1047 scopes it.
- The #661-comment fix was indeed wrong *for this class*: realpath is
  already there. And the /tmp-mirror FP has the distinct cause #1047
  names: the union match means the raw `/tmp/…/plugins/_shared` form hits
  even when its realpath leaves every governed root. One union, two
  opposite bugs — suppress the raw form and the FP dies but same-call
  writes-through-links lose their last net; keep it and both stand. The
  issue's direction (same-call `ln` whose source resolves into the closure
  → fail closed as write, the #1034 opaque-writer treatment) threads that
